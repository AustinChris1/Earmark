Money that carries
its destination.

Earmark collects money from a group chat for one named obligation, such as school fees, rent or a shared bill, and pays it straight to the wallet the group agreed on. The agent can only pay the destination it was locked to, so nothing stops at a person on the way.

Start a drive in your chat

Four moves, inside the chat.

  1. Name the obligation. /new 450 USDT 0xSchool Term 1 fees for Chioma
  2. Split it. Everyone gets their share and a one tap pay link.
  3. Money moves once. Each payment lands at the locked destination in the same transaction.
  4. The chat keeps score. Who paid, who is outstanding, all of it on chain.

No custody, by construction.

The contract has three functions that change state: createDrive, contribute and close. There is no withdraw, no owner and no upgrade path. Every contribution is forwarded to the destination inside the same transaction, so the contract never holds a balance.

Questions people ask before they pay

Does Earmark ever hold my money?
No. A payment is one transaction from your wallet to the locked destination. The contract has no balance, so there is nothing to withdraw, freeze or sweep.
Can the destination be changed after a drive opens?
No. It is written once at creation. There is no function to edit it, no owner and no upgrade path; the verified source shows this.
What happens if a drive is full, closed, or past its date?
The contract refuses the payment and nothing leaves your wallet. A drive closes itself when the target is reached.
Can I get a refund?
Not from Earmark, because it never had the money. Each contribution is a direct payment to the payee.
What does it cost?
Earmark takes no fee. You pay Celo gas, a fraction of a cent, in the stablecoin where the wallet supports fee abstraction or in CELO otherwise.
Which wallets work?
Any Celo wallet with a browser or connect button: MetaMask, Rabby, Valora, MiniPay's injected wallet. Earmark is not yet listed in MiniPay Discover.

Who it is for

  • Housemates and compounds on a shared meter, rent or water bill.
  • Small groups with a recurring levy, where a treasurer currently holds the cash.
